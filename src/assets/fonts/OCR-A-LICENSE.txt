OCR-A (ocr-a.woff2)

A digital version of the OCR-A typeface (ANSI X3.17-1977), made by John Sauter in 2004 from
Tor Lillqvist's and Richard B. Wales's work, and released by him into the public domain.

Source: https://sourceforge.net/projects/ocr-a-font/ (OCRA.ttf, version 1.0)
Licence as recorded by Debian (package fonts-ocr-a, debian/copyright):
  "This software is released into the public domain."

Traywise bundles a subset (printable ASCII), converted to WOFF2.
